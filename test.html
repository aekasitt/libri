🚀 Libri Speed Reader Test Page

How to Test:

  1. Load the extension from extension/dist/ in Chrome
  2. Select any text below
  3. Try one of these methods:
    • Click the Libri extension icon
    • Press Ctrl+Shift+L (or Cmd+Shift+L on Mac)
    • Right-click and select "Speed Read Selected Text"
  4. The speed reader overlay should appear!

Short Text Sample

Speed reading is a skill that allows you to read faster while maintaining comprehension. By displaying words one at a time, your eyes don't need to move across the page, reducing the time spent on each word.

Medium Text Sample

The Libri speed reader uses a technique called Rapid Serial Visual Presentation (RSVP). This method presents words sequentially at a fixed location on the screen. By eliminating the need for eye movement, RSVP can significantly increase reading speed. The middle letter of each word is highlighted to help your eyes focus on the optimal recognition point. Studies have shown that readers can achieve speeds of 500-1000 words per minute using this technique, compared to the average reading speed of 200-300 words per minute.

Long Text Sample

Reading is one of the most fundamental skills we develop as humans. From ancient scrolls to modern digital displays, the way we consume written information has evolved dramatically. However, the basic mechanics of reading have remained largely unchanged for centuries. We move our eyes across lines of text, word by word, line by line, page by page.

Speed reading techniques challenge this traditional approach. By presenting text in a controlled manner, these methods aim to optimize the reading process. The human brain is capable of processing information much faster than traditional reading allows. The bottleneck isn't our cognitive ability, but rather the physical limitations of eye movement and the habits we've developed over years of traditional reading.

Libri leverages modern web technologies to bring speed reading to your browser. Built with Rust and WebAssembly, it provides a fast, efficient, and privacy-focused reading experience. All processing happens locally in your browser, ensuring your reading material remains private. Whether you're reading articles, research papers, or books, Libri can help you consume content more efficiently while maintaining comprehension.

Keyboard Controls Reference

  • Space - Pause/Resume reading
  • Escape - Close the speed reader
  • ↑ - Increase speed (+30 WPM)
  • ↓ - Decrease speed (-30 WPM)
  • ← - Previous word (when paused)
  • → - Next word (when paused)

Troubleshooting:

  • Nothing happens: Open DevTools Console (F12) and check for errors
  • "Please select some text first": Make sure text is selected before activating
  • WASM not loading: Check that extension_lectio_bg.wasm exists in dist/assets/dictation-web/
  • Console logs: Look for "Libri WASM module loaded" and "Libri received message" logs
